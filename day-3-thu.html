The big nature day, and the rule for it is simple: the sightseeing sets the clock and food fits around it. You are on an island covered in places to eat — the mistake would be spending ninety minutes of daylight driving to one specific lobster pound. Every option below now assumes you eat fast and close to wherever you already are. Five ways to shape the day — coast, carriage roads, quiet side, both coasts, or the whole thing flipped so Cadillac comes first. Remember you wake up in Union, two hours west of the park, so every option is really a question about what time the alarm goes off. One rule this day rewards: start early.

Eagle Lake — carriage road entrance (post 6)ride, don't shuttle
Out Cottage St onto ME-233: the only paved, traffic-y leg, and ~350 ft of climb that the motor makes trivial. You have to ride it — the Island Explorer Bicycle Express trailers won't carry e-bikes, load limits. Gravel starts at the bridge, and that junction is signpost 6. Bubble Pond10-min stop
Ride the east shore of Eagle Lake first (posts 6→7, the flat half) — Cadillac and Pemetic rise straight out of the water — then south toward post 17, where the carriage road runs along the Bubble Pond waterline under Cadillac's west cliffs. ≈5 mi · ~15 min · food in hand by 1:10, Sand Beach by 1:25
1:30p
The Beehiveiron rungs · not for heights
Lunch is a bag, not a stop. Grab something in town on the walk back from the shop and eat it in the Jeep — that's the whole reason this starts at 1:30 instead of 2:00, and it buys the entire afternoon an extra half hour of slack.
AllTrails · The Beehive Loop ↗ — 1.6 mi loop, 508 ft, Hard, 4.9★, 1½–2 hrs. Iron rungs and ladders bolted across exposed cliff faces above Sand Beach, with several-hundred-foot drops and no guardrails. More psychological than physical, and there is no bailing partway — the route is one-way up. Trailhead is a short walk via the Bowl Trail. Dogs not allowed. Do not attempt it in rain — wet granite and wet rungs are genuinely dangerous. Name the bail-out in the parking lot, not on the rungs: anyone even slightly unsure takes Gorham Mountain instead — 1.8 mi, 525 ft, no exposure, own lot, and it's the better view of the two.
Corrected Aug 3 — parking: this card said Sand Beach is "far easier at 1:25 than at 9am." That's optimistic. In August the lot runs full from roughly 9:30a to 3:00p and turns over one car at a time. Budget fifteen minutes, not zero — roadside in the marked right lane of Park Loop past the beach is legal and normal, and the Gorham Mountain lot is the backup.
Good news: the Beehive is not part of Acadia's peregrine falcon closure despite what half the hiking blogs say — the 2026 closures are Precipice, Jordan Cliffs and Valley Cove only. Confirm same-day at nps.gov/acad conditions ↗.
Change shoes: pack hiking shoes where you can reach them in the Jeep, not buried — you're changing in a parking lot.

Cadillac Mountain summitbook a 2:00–4:00 window
Beehive first, Cadillac second — two reasons. Cadillac has a wide entry window and zero exertion, so it's the piece that absorbs slippage; you never want a reservation clock running while you're on iron rungs. Worth considering: Thunder Hole booms 1–2 hrs before high water and high tide Thursday is 5:05p — so 3:15–4:15p is the one window all week it actually performs, and it sits directly on the one-way Park Loop between Sand Beach and the summit road. Booking the 4:00 window instead of an earlier one would let you stop there around 3:30 on the way up. Your call — it pushes Freeport ~45 min later.

Acadia's only lighthouse, bolted to a granite headland. Take the stairs down to the rocks for the postcard angle. Small lot — mornings are the calm window. ~30 min. AllTrails ↗ — 0.4 mi, 62 ft, easy.
