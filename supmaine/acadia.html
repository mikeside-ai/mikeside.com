Do this first — tomorrow morning

Cadillac timed entry drops Tue Aug 4, 10:00 AM ET

Seventy percent of Thursday's summit-road slots release two days out — 10:00 AM ET tomorrow, which is the exact minute you're boarding DL0426 at Detroit. This is the only piece of Thursday that can be permanently lost, and it costs $6.

The plan: 9:50a alarm. All three of you on recreation.gov at once from the gate, logged in with payment saved before 9:55. Whoever gets through first, the others stop. Screenshot the QR — there is no cell signal on the summit road and rangers scan at the gate.

Also check right now: the 30% released back in early May often has leftovers, and a leftover today beats a scramble tomorrow.

If it sells out, you have two real fallbacks

1. Go late. The reservation covers daytime entry only — roughly 9:00a to 4:30p — and outside that window the road is open to anyone. A 4:45p drive-up needs no ticket, lands you on the summit in the best light of the day, and costs you about an hour on the Freeport arrival. Confirm the cutoff time on recreation.gov before you bet the day on it; the hours have shifted between seasons.
2. Walk up. The reservation is for vehicles. On foot you can climb the North Ridge Trail any time — 4.4 mi round trip, ~1,100 ft, open granite ridge with Frenchman Bay under you the whole way. That's a 2½-hour commitment, so it's a different day, not a swap. But it means the summit is never actually closed to you.

The ranger's take

Straight answer: Option E is the best-engineered day on the card — summit at its clearest hour, quiet side after lunch, off the island by 2:00 with the evening loose. But the e-bikes are booked and paid, so E is academic unless you're willing to eat that cost. You're running B. Keep it. Here's how I'd sharpen it.

1. Book the 4:00 Cadillac window, not 2:00 — and put Thunder Hole in front of it

High tide is 5:05p. Thunder Hole performs one to two hours before high water, which makes 3:15–4:15p Thursday the single window all week when it actually booms instead of gurgling. It sits directly on the one-way Park Loop between Sand Beach and the summit road — you drive past it anyway. Off the Beehive ~3:15, Thunder Hole 3:30, summit gate 4:00. A later window is also more forgiving if the rungs run long, and 4:30 on the summit is softer light and half the crowd.

2. Decide the ride length at the shop, not on the road

The 21-mile Eagle Lake → Jordan Pond loop is the scenic heart of the carriage roads and it returns you at 12:45 with zero slack and tired legs, forty-five minutes before iron rungs. The 13-mile Duck Brook → Witch Hole version banks a full hour and Duck Brook Bridge is the most beautiful built thing in the park. If the afternoon (Beehive + Thunder Hole + summit) is the priority, ride short. If the carriage roads are the day, ride long and trade the Beehive for Gorham Mountain. What doesn't work is riding long and assuming the afternoon holds.

3. Name the Beehive bail-out before you're standing under it

The Beehive is one-way up. Once you're on the rungs there is no turning around, no passing, and no talking someone down — I've watched that go badly more than once. Have the conversation in the parking lot: anyone who is even slightly unsure takes Gorham Mountain instead — 1.8 mi, 525 ft, moderate, same trailhead area, ocean views the whole ridge, about an hour. Nobody should discover their limit two hundred feet above Sand Beach.

Where I'd push back on the current card

It says Sand Beach parking is "far easier at 1:25 than at 9am." That's optimistic. In August that lot runs full from roughly 9:30a until 3:00p and turns over one car at a time. Your real plan is roadside parking in the right lane of Park Loop past the beach — legal and normal where the pavement is marked — or the Gorham Mountain lot with a walk back up the Bowl Trail. Budget fifteen minutes for parking, not zero.

Decision points on the day

6:15a · before you leave Union

Check the fog, not the forecast

August mornings on Mount Desert Island are fog-prone in a way the inland forecast never captures. If the summit is socked in, the carriage roads are usually below the fog layer and completely unaffected — Eagle Lake in mist is genuinely better than Eagle Lake in sun. Conversely, a rare crystal morning is an argument for pulling the summit earlier if a window is still available.

9:00a · at the bike shop

Ask two questions before you sign

Is "half day" a hard 1:00 return or a rolling four hours? That single answer decides whether the Beehive is comfortable or rushed. And ask for locks — bikes are banned from every hiking trail in the park, so any walking at all means leaving them at a junction. Sign all three waivers in the customer portal tonight; the shop's own confirmation warns that setup eats the first stretch of the morning.

11:00a · at Bubble Pond or Jordan Pond

Nobody swims

Eagle Lake, Bubble Pond and Jordan Pond are all public drinking-water supplies. Swimming and wading are prohibited and rangers do enforce it. Echo Lake is the swimming lake and it's on the far side of the island. On a hot day this catches people out.

1:25p · the Sand Beach parking lot

Fifteen minutes, not zero

Roadside in the marked right lane of Park Loop past the beach is legal and normal. Gorham Mountain lot is the backup with a walk back up the Bowl Trail. The Island Explorer shuttle is free and stops at Sand Beach — worth knowing if the lot is a circus.

1:30p · the Beehive trailhead

Look up, then decide

You can see the rungs from the bottom. That's the moment to choose, and the answer changes if the granite is wet — do not attempt it in rain or within a couple of hours of it. Gorham Mountain is the swap, and it is not a consolation prize.

3:30p · Thunder Hole

This is the hour

Ninety minutes before the 5:05p high, with an incoming swell, is when the chamber actually claps and throws spray over the railing. Stand back from the lower platform if it's running — people get soaked and the granite gets slick.

4:45p · leaving the island

Don't book dinner

Camden or Rockland around 6:30–7:00 is the natural break. Young's in Belfast is on the route but the posted hours conflict badly across sources — one recent listing closes them at 3pm. Call 207-338-1160 if it matters; otherwise the Harraseeket's own Broad Arrow Tavern runs late and is downstairs from your bed.

Field notes

🎫
Park entrance pass — $35/vehicle, seven days, separate from the Cadillac reservation. Buy it online before you arrive; your QR is on the pass page. Rangers check at the Sand Beach entrance station.
📵
Cell service is unreliable park-wide and absent on the summit road. Screenshot the Cadillac QR, the bike reservation and the Harraseeket address before you leave Union. Download the offline map area for MDI tonight.
💧
Water on the carriage roads: none until Jordan Pond House. Twenty-one miles in August heat is more than people expect. Two bottles each, minimum, plus something salty.
👟
Pack hiking shoes where you can reach them in the Jeep, not buried. You're changing in a parking lot at 1:25 with a reservation clock running.
🦅
2026 peregrine closures: Precipice, Jordan Cliffs and Valley Cove. The Beehive is not closed, whatever the hiking blogs say. Confirm same-day at nps.gov/acad conditions ↗.
🚴
Class 1 e-bikes only on the carriage roads — Class 2 and 3 are prohibited. 20 mph limit, and cyclists yield to absolutely everyone including horses. Give carriages a wide berth and don't ring at a horse.
🌡️
The summit runs 10–15° cooler and much windier than sea level. A wind layer lives in the Jeep, not at the hotel.
🪳
Ticks are active in August on the grassy carriage-road edges and the Bowl Trail. Check at the hotel — deer ticks here are small enough to miss.
