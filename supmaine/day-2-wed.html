The quintessential Midcoast day, and the one with the highest-rated single experience of the whole trip in it. Two ways to spend the morning — out on the water aboard a 1927 schooner, or up Mount Battie on foot. The afternoon is the same either way: a mile-long granite breakwater walk and a golden-hour lighthouse cameo, then a campfire, alpacas, and some of the darkest stargazing skies on the coast.

11:00a
Mount Battie hike
Two ways up on foot, and they are not the same hike. The direct Mount Battie Trail is short but genuinely steep — 1.2 mi round trip, 593 ft of gain, AllTrails rates it hard, with a rock scramble near the top. The Carriage Road route reaches the same summit at a walkable grade — 2.4 mi, 564 ft, moderate, about 75 min. Or skip the climb and drive the auto road, ~$12/vehicle. Same postcard view over the harbor either way.
