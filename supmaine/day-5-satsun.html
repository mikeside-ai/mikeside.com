Option A — Walk Portland from the hotel · flat peninsula loop, no car, coffee never far
10:00a
Old Port cobblestones + coffee
Straight out the hotel door into the Old Port — Exchange & Fore St, brick and cobblestone, harbor shops. Grab a coffee (Tandem, Bard) and ease in. You start where you're standing.
≈0.5 mi on foot · ~10 min along the harbor
walk
Commercial St + the working waterfront
Stroll the wharves and fish piers along Casco Bay — ferries coming and going, lobster boats, the odd schooner.
lunch
Gilbert's Chowder Housechowder
Right on the walk at 92 Commercial St — a casual Old Port institution overlooking Custom House Wharf. Award-winning Maine clam chowder, lobster stew, and lobster rolls; counter-casual, ~$20–30, open ~11a–9p daily. The perfect low-key recovery lunch, ~5 min from the hotel.
≈1 mi on foot · ~20 min east to Munjoy Hill
walk
Eastern Promenade & Fort Allen Park
Grassy bluff over Casco Bay and the islands — the best free view in the city. Drop to the shoreline path (the Eastern Prom Trail) and climb back up Munjoy Hill.
optional +3.5 mi · ~1 hr flat loop
option
Back Cove Trailadd-on
If the legs are willing: a flat 3.6-mi gravel loop around the cove with the skyline across the water — 29 ft of elevation, about an hour, and the easiest thing on this whole site. Otherwise loop back through Congress St (galleries, the Portland Museum of Art) to the hotel. Whole walk ≈ 3–5 mi, all flat.

In town: Eastern Prom Trail + Back Cove loop
Flat, paved, right on the water — the Eastern Prom Trail along Casco Bay linked to the Back Cove loop, ~6–7 mi round trip with skyline and island views. Cross to South Portland for the Bug Light / Spring Point lighthouses if you want more.
≈10–16 mi · ~1–1.5 hr out-and-back
ride
The marshes: Eastern Trail to Scarborough Marsh
Head south on the Eastern Trail — a flat rail-trail through Maine's biggest salt marsh, herons and egrets, boardwalk stretches. Ride as far as the legs want (10–16 mi round trip) and turn back. It's the same marsh as Monday's ride, so save it for one day or the other.
