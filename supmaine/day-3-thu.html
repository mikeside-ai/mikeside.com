The big nature day, built around a lobster lunch — Thurston's out on Bass Harbor if you're on the quiet side, Bar Harbor Lobster Pound at 11:00 sharp if you're not. Five ways to shape it — coast, carriage roads, quiet side, both coasts, or the whole day flipped so Cadillac comes first. Remember you wake up in Union, two hours west of the park, so every option below is really a question about what time the alarm goes off. One rule this day rewards: start early.

Lunch: Thurston's Lobster Pound, Bernardno reservations
9 Thurston Rd, awning-covered decks over Bass Harbor, boats unloading the catch you're about to eat. Order at the counter, take a buzzer. Pre-cracked lobster, scallop chowder, steamers, corn, blueberry cake. Heads up: some seasons they open at noon and a queue forms before the doors — call the week of, and if it's a noon open, either be there 11:45 or slide to 12:30. Budget ~75 min, out by 1:15.
11:00a
Swap for A & B: Bar Harbor Lobster PoundA & B only
414 ME-3, on the north side by the Hulls Cove entrance — which is to say, ten minutes from the east-side stops instead of forty-five. Opens 11:00a daily, order at the window, line moves fast. 4.7★ over 112 reviews, #15 of 103 in Bar Harbor, Travelers' Choice 2025; lobster rolls $30–33. Reviews all say the same thing: it gets crushed at dinner, so 11:00 is the zero-wait slot. If you take Option A or B, this saves the 45-min haul out to Bernard and back and buys you most of an hour — Thurston's is the better room, but it is only worth the drive if you're already on the quiet side (C, D or E).
