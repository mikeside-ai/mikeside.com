The big nature day, now built around lunch at Thurston's on Bass Harbor. Three ways to spend the morning — coast, carriage roads, or the quiet west side — and they all land on the same dock at noon. Everything after is fixed: Cadillac at 2, then the long haul south. One rule this day rewards: start early.

Several shops rent Class 1 e-bikes (the only class allowed on carriage roads) — Bar Harbor Bicycle Shop, Acadia SUP & E-Bike, Island Time. August fleets book out; reserve online days ahead. Ask for the earliest pickup they'll do.

Honest trade-off: adding Thurston's costs the Jordan Pond leg of the ride, so this becomes a good hour and a half instead of a great three. If the ride is the point, do the full Eagle Lake→Jordan Pond loop, eat popovers at 11:45, and put lobster back on Young's deck in Belfast at 5:30 instead. Also note: this morning claims the same tide window as a Bar Island crossing (low tide 10:48a) — can't do both.

Bass Harbor Head Light
Acadia's only lighthouse, bolted to a granite headland. Take the stairs down to the rocks for the postcard angle. Small lot — mornings are the calm window. ~30 min.

Two flat 1.2–1.4 mi loops a mile apart, both ending on open granite shore with tidepools. Do one or both. This is the Acadia nobody photographs — spruce, ledge, and nothing but water south of you until Nova Scotia.

Every way · lunch on the wharf, then the afternoon is fixed
12:00p
Lunch: Thurston's Lobster Pound, Bernardno reservations
9 Thurston Rd, on the wharf over Bass Harbor — awning-covered decks right on the water, boats unloading the catch you're about to eat. Order at the counter, they hand you a buzzer. Pre-cracked lobster, scallop chowder, steamers, corn, blueberry cake. Seasonal (Memorial Day–mid Oct); hours run roughly 11a–8p but some seasons open at noon and skip Mondays — worth a call the week of. Budget ~75 min, out by 1:15.

Four miles from the table and directly on the way back — grab it now if you didn't take Option C. Adds ~20 min door to door, which means booking the 2:30 Cadillac window instead of the 2:00.
≈26 mi · ~50 min back east and up to Cadillac
2:00p
Cadillac Mountain summitbook the 2:00–2:30 window
Timed vehicle entry — $6 on recreation.gov, 30-min entry window, stay as long as you like. The Thurston's lunch pushes this back from 1:30, so book the 2:00 or 2:30 slot, not the 1:30. Highest point on the North Atlantic seaboard. Save the QR code offline — rangers scan it and cell service is spotty. (Sunset variant: the 7:30p slot catches sunset, at the cost of a ~11pm Freeport arrival.)
