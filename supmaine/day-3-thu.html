The big nature day, built around a lobster lunch, and there are two pounds worth eating at — Thurston's out on Bass Harbor, or Bar Harbor Lobster Pound on Route 3 the moment it opens at 11:00. Both are on the card below; they just cost different amounts of clock. Five ways to shape it — coast, carriage roads, quiet side, both coasts, or the whole day flipped so Cadillac comes first. Remember you wake up in Union, two hours west of the park, so every option below is really a question about what time the alarm goes off. One rule this day rewards: start early.

Eagle Lake — carriage road entranceride, don't shuttle
Out Cottage St onto ME-233: the only paved, traffic-y leg, and ~350 ft of climb that the motor makes trivial. You have to ride it — the Island Explorer Bicycle Express trailers won't carry e-bikes, load limits. Gravel starts at the bridge. Class 1 e-bikes are legal on all 45 mi of Acadia carriage roads (Class 2 and 3 are not), 20 mph limit, cyclists yield to everyone. Stay north of Jordan Pond — the Land & Garden Preserve carriage roads around Little Long Pond ban bikes of every kind.

10:45a
Bubble Pond10-min stop
Ride the east shore of Eagle Lake first — Cadillac and Pemetic rise straight out of the water — then south to Bubble Pond, where the carriage road runs along the waterline under Cadillac's west cliffs. Quietest spot on the route and most riders blow straight past it. No swimming: Eagle Lake, Bubble Pond and Jordan Pond are all public drinking supplies, swimming and wading prohibited. Echo Lake is the swimming lake and it's on the far side of the island.

2:00p
The Beehiveiron rungs · not for heights
AllTrails · The Beehive Loop ↗ — 1.6 mi loop, 508 ft, Hard, 4.9★, 1½–2 hrs. Iron rungs and ladders bolted across exposed cliff faces above Sand Beach, with several-hundred-foot drops and no guardrails. More psychological than physical, and there is no bailing partway — the route is one-way up. Park at Sand Beach (easier at 1:50 than at 9am); trailhead is a short walk via the Bowl Trail. Dogs not allowed. Do not attempt it in rain — wet granite and wet rungs are genuinely dangerous.
Good news: the Beehive is not part of Acadia's peregrine falcon closure despite what half the hiking blogs say — the 2026 closures are Precipice, Jordan Cliffs and Valley Cove only. Confirm same-day at nps.gov/acad conditions ↗.
Change shoes: pack hiking shoes where you can reach them in the Jeep, not buried — you're changing in a parking lot.

Every way except B · two lobster pounds — pick one, then the afternoon is fixed
12:00p
Lunch option 1: Thurston's Lobster Pound, Bernardno reservations
9 Thurston Rd, awning-covered decks over Bass Harbor, boats unloading the catch you're about to eat. Order at the counter, take a buzzer. Pre-cracked lobster, scallop chowder, steamers, corn, blueberry cake. Heads up: some seasons they open at noon and a queue forms before the doors — call the week of, and if it's a noon open, either be there 11:45 or slide to 12:30. Budget ~75 min, out by 1:15. Fits best with C, D and E, which put you out on the quiet side anyway — from the east side it is a 45-min haul each way, which is the whole trade.
11:00a
Lunch option 2: Bar Harbor Lobster Poundopens 11:00
414 ME-3, on the north side by the Hulls Cove entrance — which is to say, ten minutes from the east-side stops instead of forty-five. Opens 11:00a daily, order at the window, line moves fast. 4.7★ over 112 reviews, #15 of 103 in Bar Harbor, Travelers' Choice 2025; lobster rolls $30–33. Reviews all say the same thing: it gets crushed at dinner, so 11:00 is the zero-wait slot. Works with any of the five options, and on A and B it buys back most of an hour. The honest read: Thurston's is the better room, this is the better clock, and either one is a proper Maine lobster lunch — decide the morning of, based on how the day is actually running.
