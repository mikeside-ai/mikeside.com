Flat rail-trail through Maine's biggest saltmarsh — herons, egrets, boardwalk stretches. Out-and-back as far as legs want — the full Scarborough-to-Saco run is 8.2 mi one way with only 167 ft of climb, which on e-bikes is nothing.

Timber Point — walk to the islandtide-locked
Rachel Carson refuge, ~15 min back north. Easy 2.4-mi trail — flat, ~45 min of walking — out to a rocky bar you cross on foot at low tide. Low is 3:18p on Aug 10, so the bar is walkable roughly 1:20–5:20p and a 2:30p arrival lands right in the middle of the window. Cross, wander Timber Island, be back over the bar before the water is. No dogs on this one.
