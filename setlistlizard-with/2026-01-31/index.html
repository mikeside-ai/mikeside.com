⁴ Tease: Weekapaug Groove

⁵ After a somewhat anemic and imperfect composed section, "My Friend, My Friend" launches into extended improvisation yet again. Grimy murk-rock takes center stage initially, before amorphously gliding into a more melodic, patient low key psychedelic rock passage. This then morphs into the final act: a patiently wound up, somewhat mellow bliss-rock segment with melodious, laser-tone Trey guitar peaks. Longest version to date.

⁶ Tease: The Final Hurrah

⁷ Tease: P. Funk (Wants to Get Funked Up) by Parliament

⁸ It may already be cliched to observe the band is relaxed on the beach, but this very "soundchecky" outing is truly not in a hurry to get anywhere at all in particular, loping along nonchalantly for more than twenty minutes before it, almost as an afterthought, takes a turn for the funky and intergalactic. Pedal play ensues, intensity increases, and an effect infused, rocket powered dance party breaks out that finally gives way to a big melodic launch and gnarly peak that grinds and dissolves to -> BASOS.

⁹ Tease: We're Off to See the Wizard by Harold Arlen

¹⁰ As if inspired by the massive reading of "A Song I Heard the Ocean Sing" that preceded it, this take spreads itself out at a luxuriating, gradual pace. Melodic and rhythmic elasticity mercurially envelop the music, as is custom for "Sea of Stars", particularly colored by Fishman's finesse-driven rhythmic flux as well as Trey's both clean and affected textural playing. Play then forms up behind a spaced-out minor key drive, sounding almost threateningly similar to "ASIHTOS", building up to smokey, hazy peak before > "Meatstick".

¹¹ Trey thanks the crowd
