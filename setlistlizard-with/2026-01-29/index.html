⁴ Every time you wonder what "Ruby Waves" has left to say you get a version like this. Building upon loops and a driving riff, Trey leads the band into an uplifting peaking jam that hits the summit at 1630 via aggressive chord swells, only to find another gear and peaking with swirling madness some two minutes later. Akin to the Mexico 2024 "CDT" there's a relentlessness to this jam that rewards repeat spins. Dig the 8/30/24 "Ruby" call back as Trey pushes through Fishman's return to the song, aligning with bizarre alien invasion tones from Page and Mike, and launching this jam into the ether. Spellbinding stuff, sometimes the band is just so locked in. Breaking down around 26min we fade out via drones into "Lonely Trip."
