What each night taught

#92 established run no-repeat — the played nights share zero songs. Still the single most valuable rule, and free.

#93 exposed a measurement error in the model since v4: ranking songs inside the full theme-year pool instead of the eligible post-burn pool. Corrected, hit density falls monotonically with rank — no mid-depth sweet spot.

#94 produced the costume-album rule and the frequency-versus-narrative split.

The costume-album rule

Phish's Halloween “musical costume” tradition — covering a whole album — ran from 1994 until they began writing their own with Wingsuit/Fuego. It fires for exactly the years that have one: 1992 none → nothing appeared. 1993 none → nothing appeared. 1994 White Album → Back in the U.S.S.R. opened. 1995 is Quadrophenia, 1996 is Remain in Light. A play-count model structurally cannot find these songs — one or two plays in the theme year puts them at the bottom of every frequency list.

Called shots, reframed after 0-for-4

Opener is now the costume track-one pick rather than the frequency leader, which backtested at rank 14 and 3 and then missed entirely. Encore is now the Fish/novelty slot. Longest jam follows era-narrative over jamchart count. Bustout is now a count, not a name — 3, 4 and 3 songs past a 300-show gap across the three nights, while no individual candidate clears 10%.

Still true

Do not filter on dormancy — every night runs a current-rotation core plus 3–4 deep bustouts. Secret Language is collinear with play count. Skipping the obvious loses hits on the bulk slate every way it is cut.
