What a test is, and why these are honest

Every model is a deterministic rule executed blind — no hand-picking, no knowledge of the setlist. Play counts come only from shows strictly before the target date, so nothing leaks backwards. The burn list is the actual set of songs played on earlier nights of that same run.

What “Chance” means: the comparator is 20 blind picks from the identical eligible pool — pure arithmetic, not a model. Across the hundred shows that expects 309 hits; Forbin v10 got 836, Forbin v9 828, Forbin v7 806, Forbin v8 765.

What this changes

1. Forbin v10 is released and leads at 836. Its entire gain sits on the 28 special-occasion shows, where it beats Forbin v9 by 8 songs and cuts calibration error from 1.56 to 1.24. On the other 72 shows it is Forbin v9, exactly.

2. Forbin v9's rotation term was actively hurting occasion nights. It scored 298 there against plain Forbin v7's 303 — a curated residency does not avoid recently-played songs, and penalising them cost real hits. Forbin v10 switches it off for those nights only.

3. Forbin v8 is settled, and it is a loss. 765 against Forbin v7's 806 — 41 songs given up, 43 shows lost to 11 won. Three independent measurements agree. Reserved slots are gone.

4. Every clever ranking idea has failed except one. Cover quotas, reserved slots, mid-depth barbells and the short-song filler index all lost to plain frequency. Only Forbin v9's rotation term survived a holdout, and only on ordinary shows. The frequency core does the work; the real gains have come from being honest about the odds.

5. The ceiling has not moved. Roughly 60% of what Phish plays still sits outside any frequency rule's reach, and 368 of 2,281 songs were unreachable in principle. Getting past that needs a different signal — segue affinity and set-position priors are next.

Patch notes

Forbin v10released27 Jul 2026

Occasion-aware. Two changes, both confined to special-occasion nights — NYE, Halloween, Baker's Dozen and this MSG run.

  • Rotation-state term switched off on curated nights. A residency does not avoid recently-played songs; the only songs out of play are the ones burned earlier in the same run. On the 28 occasion shows Forbin v9 was scoring below plain Forbin v7 (298 against 303) because of this. Forbin v10 scores 306.
  • Probabilities lifted 1.4× on occasion nights, capped at 78%. Those nights average 27.1 songs against 21.1, so every pick is likelier to land. Calibration goes 1.56 → 1.24. No picks change.
  • Tested and rejected: a “shorter songs” ranking bias built on a filler index. It finds the right songs but gives no edge — see chart 8.
Forbin v9superseded27 Jul 2026

Added rotation state to the frequency core, and fixed the odds.

  • Rotation-state multiplier. A song played recently is about half as likely as its play rate suggests. Worth +6.7% on the ladder across 100 shows.
  • Calibration curve fitted on 50 shows, validated on 50 more. Raw play rates understate reality by 2.05×.
  • Reserved slots removed — measured losing three separate times.
  • A first draft built on an apparent sign-flip in the rotation signal failed its holdout and was discarded. The flip was an artifact of a 10-show sample.
Forbin v8retired25 Jul 2026

Forbin v7 plus three reserved slots: costume album, Fish novelty, most era-locked song.

  • Measured losing three times — on #94, on a 10-show backtest, and on 100 simulations, where it finished 41 songs behind Forbin v7 and lost head-to-head 43 shows to 11.
  • The slots buy genuinely unreachable songs and pay about four picks for every one that lands.
Forbin v7the baseline that would not die25 Jul 2026

Top 20 of the eligible pool by theme-year play count. The whole model.

  • Caught 806 of 2,281 across 100 simulations, 2.61× chance.
  • Catches every played song inside the top-20 rank band and none outside it — the rule and the band are the same object. That band holds only 40% of what Phish actually plays.
Forbin v6retired25 Jul 2026

Frequency head plus a cover quota and archetype buckets.

  • Best cover-catcher of any model — 193 of 444 — and still finished 107 songs behind Forbin v7.
  • Reserving slots for a category reliably improves that category and reliably costs more than it returns.
Forbin v5retired24 Jul 2026

Mid-depth barbell: top 8 plus twelve from the 20–40% depth band.

  • The only model published blind before the backtests existed. Scored 6/20 live on #93.
  • 592 across 100 simulations — last place, 1.92× chance.
