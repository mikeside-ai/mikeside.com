🕰️ The '94 night — a Madison Square Garden Tweezerfest nodding to 5/7/94 at the Bomb Factory: Tweezer surfacing six times before the Reprise encore.
Set 2 wove Tweezer in and out of Down with Disease, Sample in a Jar, Catapult and Cavern, Bomb Factory-style; the Purple Rain encore completed the '94 homage. Wolfman's Brother ended mid-song in Set 1 after a front-of-house power outage.
