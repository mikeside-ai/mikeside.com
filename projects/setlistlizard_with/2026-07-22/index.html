Bustout city: Glide, Cold as Ice — unheard since 5/18/92 — and The Vibration of Life narration, last given voice in 1996, woven into David Bowie. Axilla was played '92-style with no Part II outro; Mike's Song brought the trampolines back, and I Am Hydrogen the bicycle kicks.
Community pulse — called an instant classic, "all-killer, no-filler." Fish's vacuum turn in Love You and the Bouncing Around the Room jam drew special mention.
