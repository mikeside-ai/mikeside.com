Two trends are pulling in opposite directions. Speed is genuinely improving — moving pace has come down from 12:51 /mi in early June to 7:30 /mi on Jul 19, and the last two runs both cleared the 8:17 /mi that the predicted 25:45 5K implies. Volume has gone the other way: the Jun 22 peak week was 7.7 miles, the last four weeks average 2.9, and the current week is empty at seven days since any recorded session.

For a stated VO2 max build, the shape of that is backwards. Short and fast is the right stimulus, but it only compounds on top of an aerobic base, and the base is where the miles went. The other thing worth fixing: FTP is an estimate rather than a measured number, and the 5K time is a prediction rather than a result, so every zone on this page is derived from a race you have not actually run. One timed 5K would replace all of it with real data.
