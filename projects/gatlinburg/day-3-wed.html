The plan

Plan A

The 11-mile loop through the valley with the deer, turkeys, old churches and sometimes a bear. An hour-plus each way from the cabin on Little River Road, so you leave early and you are back for naps.

  • 7:15a
    Roll out, breakfast in the car ★ 4.74k Directions
    Cades Cove Loop Rd via Little River Rd and Laurel Creek Rd
    Little River Road from Sugarlands. Wildlife is out at this hour and the loop is empty. Leave at nine and you crawl behind forty cars.
  • 8:30a
    Start the loop Directions
    One way, 20 mph, pull-offs everywhere. Get out at John Oliver Cabin, half a mile flat from the road. Cash counts deer.
  • 10:00a
    Cable Mill area, halfway round ★ 4.74k Directions
    Cades Cove Visitor Center, Cable Mill
    Visitor center, restrooms, a working grist mill and barns on flat gravel. Stroller works here. Thirty minutes of running around, then back in the car.
  • 11:15a
    Finish the loop, head home Directions
    Metcalf Bottoms picnic area, Little River Rd
    Lunch in the car or at the Metcalf Bottoms picnic area on the way back if it's warm enough.
  • 1:00p
    Home, naps
    Long nap. Everyone, including adults.
  • 3:30p
    Thanksgiving grocery run ★ 4.49k Directions
    1219 East Parkway
    Food City on East Parkway for tomorrow's dinner. Wednesday afternoon is the last sane time to do it. Food City runs holiday hours Thursday.
  • 5:00p
    Optional: the Arts & Crafts show ★ 4.62k Directions
    234 Historic Nature Trail
    Great Smoky Thanksgiving Arts & Crafts Show at the Gatlinburg Convention Center, Nov 24 to 29. Free, indoor, warm, and an easy hour if the kids are restless. Skip it if they aren't.

Bears are real in the Cove in November

They are fattening up before denning and they are on the loop. Fifty yards minimum, nobody gets out of the car for a bear, and no food left out at the picnic area. If a bear jam forms, drive through it. You will see another one.

Plan B: Little River Road closed, or sleet

Check the park road status link on the hub before you leave. Ice closes Little River Road and the Cove loop with no notice. If it's shut, stay on the Gatlinburg side.

  • 8:30a
    Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail ★ 4.83k Directions
    Turn at traffic light #8 onto Historic Nature Trail. Five and a half one-way miles, old cabins, the Noah Ogle place a hundred yards from the road. Closes for winter Monday, so this is its last week too.
  • 10:30a
    Gatlinburg Trail from Sugarlands ★ 4.610 Directions
    Two miles each way on gravel along the river, flat, stroller fine. Turn around whenever.
  • 12:30p
    Home, lunch, naps
  • 3:30p
    Thanksgiving grocery run ★ 4.49k Directions
    Food City, same as Plan A. This does not move.

Bail-out: Cades Cove Visitor Center at Cable Mill ★ 4.74k Directions

Halfway round the loop. Real restrooms, a ranger, a covered porch. If the loop is jammed or a kid is done, this is where you regroup, and the back half of the loop is faster than the front.
