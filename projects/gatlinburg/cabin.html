Sunday

Getting in

Check-in is 4pm sharp, smart lock

The door code arrives in the Vrbo app before check-in. Screenshot it before you enter the park at Townsend, because there is no signal on Little River Road and the app won't load at the door. Save the address for offline directions at the same time. If the lock doesn't take the code, message the host in the app from the driveway; the cabin is in town, so the phone works there.

Parking and the road

Two cars at the cabin. The second car goes in the grass at the bottom of the driveway, nose out. The cabin sits low and inside the city limits, so there's no mountain road to worry about if it ices; the one thing to watch is the driveway itself on a frosty morning, and that's a carry-nobody-down-it rule, not a driving rule.

Order of operations on arrival: one adult does the sweep while the other keeps all three kids in the car or on the lawn. Then unload. Then groceries go away before anyone opens the deck door. Food City is on the way in, so the cold stuff is already in the car.

Neighbors

Bears at the cabin

Reviewers see bears from this porch most weeks, and late November is when they're eating everything they can find before denning. The rules are simple and the kids can learn them on the drive up.

TrashInside, lid on, until it goes to the can on checkout morning. Nothing on the deck, ever, including the grill drip tray.
CarsLocked, and no food or snack bags left in them overnight. Gatlinburg bears open unlocked car doors; it's a local sport.
DeckNo kid on the deck alone, and nobody on it at dusk without a look first. Hot tub time is before dark.
If one's in the yardEveryone inside, door shut, watch from the window. Do not feed it, photograph it from the glass, and let it leave. It will.
If one won't leaveGatlinburg police non-emergency, or the host. A bear that stays is a bear somebody fed.
For CashBear job: he's the one who checks the trash lid is on before bed. Real job, real responsibility, counts toward the seven bears on the hub.

The week

Living in it

Naps and nights

Cabins creak and the porch light stays on all night. Sound machines in both kid rooms, trash bags and painter's tape on the bunk room window, and the stair gate goes across the bunk room door the moment Slavik is down. The long nap is 12:30 to 2:30 every day and every plan on this site bends around it.

Hot tub rules

Adults and Cash only, and Cash for ten minutes at a time with an adult in the water. Slavik and Snejana do not go in; the water is too hot for a body that size. Cover back on and strapped the moment the last adult climbs out, before the towel. One adult is the named watcher whenever the deck door is open, and you hand that job off out loud.

Thermostat lives at 68 and the host checks it at checkout, so leave it there. The oven is small and runs hot, so test it Wednesday night with the sides before it meets the turkey breast on Thursday. Run the dishwasher every night; it doubles as the checkout list.
