The plan

Plan A

  • 7:00a
    Breakfast, load, sweep
    Check under every bed, the hot tub deck, and the loft. Loveys get left in cabins the same way they get left at beach houses. Bears do not take them. Probably. Then the host's list, which they grade you on: dishwasher loaded and started, used towels in the tub or shower, leftover food and trash out, thermostat set to 68, lights off, doors locked. Checkout is 10; you're out by 8:30.
  • 8:30a ET
    Roll out through the park Directions
    Little River Rd to Townsend Wye, then US-321
    Little River Road to Townsend, US-321 to Lenoir City, I-75 south. Saturday after Thanksgiving is the second heaviest checkout day of the year on the Spur and TN-66. The park route misses all of it.
  • 11:15a ET
    Lunch in Athens or Cleveland Directions
  • 11:45a CT
    Chattanooga, back on I-59. You just got an hour back
  • 2:30p CT
    Home

Plan B: ice on Little River Road

A hard freeze overnight can close Little River Road or make it a bad idea with kids in the car. Then you take the four-lane way out and accept the traffic.

  • 8:30a ET
    The Spur to Pigeon Forge, Parkway north Directions
    Gatlinburg Bypass off the Spur if downtown is moving slowly. Then the Pigeon Forge Parkway all the way through.
  • 9:15a ET
    TN-66 north to I-40 at exit 407 Directions
    This is the stretch everyone warns about. On the Saturday after Thanksgiving it can be an hour for ten miles. Leaving before 8:30 is the only trick.
  • 10:00a ET
    I-40 west, I-75 south Directions
    Through Knoxville. Lunch at Athens or Cleveland as planned.
  • 2:45p CT
    Home
    About 20 minutes longer than Plan A on a clear day. Longer if 66 is bad.

Bail-out: Townsend, if the car won't take Little River Road Directions

If anyone is sick before the Wye, Townsend has gas, a pharmacy and a quiet park on the river. Regroup there, then US-321 to Maryville is all easy road.
