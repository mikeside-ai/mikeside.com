Eleven tracks, one seven-year-old

Mountain coasters

Every alpine coaster within 35 minutes of the cabin, what each one costs, who can ride it, and the three worth the drive.

Chris Hagerman · CC BY-SA 3.0
Sun 22Mon 23Tue 24Wed 25Thu 26Fri 27Sat 28BonusTrailsFishingCabinBearsCoasters

Gatlinburg, Pigeon Forge and Sevierville have more mountain coasters than anywhere in the country: a sled on a rail, you work the brakes, a lift hill up and a run down through the trees. Two of them are already on the plan (Ober on Thursday, Anakeesta on Tuesday) and a third sits beside Monday's stop at The Island. This page is the rest of the field, ranked for this crew, because the rules decide who rides and the differences between tracks are bigger than the brochures admit.

Who can ride, this week

Cash rides every one of them as the front passenger with an adult driving; at seven he's over the 38-inch passenger line everywhere and under the 54 to 56 inches needed to drive. He can't ride alone anywhere this trip. Slavik is the problem: nearly every coaster's rule is age 3 and 38 inches, and he's two until December 28. He's past the height, but the rule is the rule, so plan on him feeding goats or riding the carousel while Cash rides, and treat any coaster that lets him on as a bonus, not a promise. Ober's note on Thursday has been changed to say so. Snejana doesn't ride. Rail Runner at Anakeesta has a 230-pound combined weight cap that rules out most adult-and-kid pairs; everything else is 375 pounds, 330 in rain.

Before you buy

The rules that bite

Passenger line38 inches and age 3 nearly everywhere. Height alone isn't enough; staff ask the age.
Driver line54 to 56 inches and usually 16+. Cash drives nothing this trip, so every ride is a two-seater with one of you.
Weight375 lb combined dry, 330 lb in rain, on the Wiegand tracks. Ober is 300. Rail Runner is 230.
RainMost run in rain with shields and the lower weight cap. Ober's Flyer and Rail Runner close. Everything closes for lightning and ice, and most give a rain check good for a year.
Re-ridesKeep the ticket or receipt. Nearly every track sells a same-day second ride for about $10, which is where the value is.
Lockers and hatsNo loose phones, hats or hoods. Ripley's pulls you from the line for it; others are looser. Zip pockets.
NightAll of them light the track after dark and it's a different ride. Dark is 5:25, so a 6pm coaster is a night ride with a bedtime.
ColdThirty mph at 40 degrees is a wind chill in the twenties. Gloves, a hat that stays on under the hood, and the fleece zipped. Ten minutes on the lift hill is the cold part.
