The plan

Plan A

Load the car Saturday night. Kids ride in pajamas and change at the first stop. Check-in is 4pm sharp with a smart lock, so there is no reason to leave before seven. The code comes through the Vrbo app; screenshot it before you enter the park, where there is no signal.

  • 7:00a CT
    Pull out
    Breakfast in the car. Snejana should sleep the first leg. I-59 north.
  • 8:40a CT
    Fort Payne, first stretch Directions
    Exit 218, Glenn Blvd. The MAPCO is right off the ramp. Bathrooms, gas, ten minutes of running around. Last stop in Central time.
  • 10:30a ET
    Chattanooga, onto I-75 north
    Clocks jump an hour here. Reset the car clock now so nobody does arrival math wrong later.
  • 11:20a ET
    Athens or Sweetwater, real stop Directions
    Exit 49. Cracker Barrel at the ramp has the fastest kids' lunch on this route and a rocking-chair porch to burn ten minutes on. Stay off the exits closer to Knoxville, they are busier and slower.
  • 12:15p ET
    Exit 81, Lenoir City. US-321 east Directions
    Love's at the exit for the last cheap tank. Then US-321 through Maryville to Townsend, about an hour.
  • 1:30p ET
    Into the park at the Townsend Wye Directions
    Little River Road to Sugarlands, 35 minutes if nobody stops. No cell signal inside the park, so have the cabin directions saved offline before you enter.
  • 2:15p ET
    Food City, 1219 East Parkway ★ 4.49k Directions
    Full stock-up before the cabin. One adult shops, one adult walks kids around the lot. If you came in via Wears Valley instead, Kroger at 220 Wears Valley Rd in Pigeon Forge is right on the route.
  • 3:30p ET
    Kill time until check-in ★ 4.7878 Directions
    Mynatt Park, Asbury Ln
    Mynatt Park on Airport Rd, five minutes from downtown. Creek, picnic tables, clean restrooms. Reviewers note there's no playground; the creek is the playground. Don't sit in the car.
  • 4:00p ET
    Into the cabin. Safety sweep first
    Before anything is unloaded: hot tub cover strapped and locked, deck doors latched, stair gate up, loft rail checked, fireplace blocked. See the note below. Then unload. Parking is two cars at the cabin; the second car goes in the grass at the bottom of the driveway. The cabin is inside the city limits and low, so no mountain road to worry about in weather.
  • 5:30p ET
    Dinner in. Early
    Frozen pizza night. Everybody is wrecked and it is already dark.
  • 7:00p ET
    Everyone down
    Pack-n-play in the darkest room, sound machines on, trash bags over any window facing the porch light. It is 6pm to their bodies, which helps this one night.

Who sleeps where

Three bedrooms, sleeps ten. Downstairs king with its own bath and the clawfoot tub: the grandparents, and it doubles as the kids' bath. Upstairs king: Vlad and Allison, pack-n-play in the closet corner for Snejana. Queen log bunk room: Cash on top, Slavik on the bottom with the stair gate across the door. Loft futon: spare, and the loft rail is on the safety sweep. The dining table seats six and there are seven of you, so bring the booster and the travel high chair; neither is listed. Washer and dryer are in the cabin, so pack light on kid clothes. Reviewers see bears from the porch most weeks: trash goes inside, never on the deck, and no kid on the deck alone.

Plan B: Wears Valley instead of the park road

Anyone carsick, Little River Road closed, or you are running late and want four lanes as long as possible.

  • 12:15p ET
    Exit 81, Lenoir City. US-321 east Directions
    Same as Plan A through Maryville to Townsend.
  • 1:20p ET
    Townsend, stay on US-321 Directions
    Do not turn into the park. US-321 becomes Wears Valley Road and climbs over the ridge. Gentler curves, four miles shorter, and cell signal the whole way.
  • 1:50p ET
    Kroger, 220 Wears Valley Rd Directions
    Right where Wears Valley Road meets Pigeon Forge. Do the full stock-up here instead of Food City.
  • 2:45p ET
    Parkway south to the Spur Directions
    Traffic light #3 in Pigeon Forge. On a holiday Sunday budget 45 minutes for the next 12 miles. Take the Gatlinburg Bypass off the Spur to skip downtown entirely.
  • 3:30p ET
    Mynatt Park until check-in ★ 4.7878 Directions
    Same stall as Plan A. Playground, creek, bathrooms.

Bail-out: Pilot Travel Center, exit 81 Directions

If the car or a kid falls apart between Chattanooga and Townsend, this is the big clean stop with a real restroom block and hot food. Everything after it is two-lane.
