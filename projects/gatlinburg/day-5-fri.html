Up the mountain, away from everyone

Friday, Nov 27 Black Friday

Black Friday. Every parking lot on the Parkway is full by ten. You go the other direction: 13 miles up Newfound Gap Road, 7 more to Kuwohi on its last weekend open, and back down for naps while the town fights over outlet parking.

The plan

Plan A

The Parkway, the Island, Dollywood and the outlets will be the busiest they are all year. Do not go near any of it. Kuwohi Road closes Monday for the winter. This is the last chance.

  • 8:30a
    Newfound Gap Road south from Sugarlands Directions
    US-441 south from Sugarlands
    Thirteen miles climbing to Newfound Gap at 5,046 feet, then seven miles of Kuwohi Road. Dress everyone for 35 degrees and wind regardless of what the cabin thermometer says. If the road is gated for ice, turn around; there's no second route.
  • 9:30a
    Kuwohi (Clingmans Dome) Directions
    End of Kuwohi Rd, 7 mi off Newfound Gap
    Half a mile paved and steep to the tower. Not a stroller walk. Snejana in the carrier, Slavik on shoulders for the second half. The highest point in Tennessee, and Cash can say he walked to it.
  • 10:45a
    Newfound Gap on the way down Directions
    Newfound Gap overlook, state line
    Stand on the state line. Restrooms here. Chimneys picnic area lower down if it's warm enough to sit outside.
  • 12:30p
    Home, lunch, naps
  • 3:30p
    Start the pack-out
    Pull the muddy gear onto the porch, run laundry, box what won't get used again. Saturday morning gets ugly otherwise.
  • 5:45p
    Shadrack's drive-through lights, Sevierville Directions
    Soaky Mountain Waterpark, 175 Soaky Way, Sevierville
    At Soaky Mountain Waterpark, $39.95 per car Friday, opens at 6, rain or shine. Kids stay in their car seats in pajamas with the radio synced to the lights. Forty minutes, then they're asleep before you're back. Sevierville traffic on Black Friday is the price. Leave early or skip it.

If the high road is closed

Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail is the fallback, and it also closes Monday. Turn at traffic light #8 onto Historic Nature Trail, five and a half miles one way, narrow and slow, past the Noah Ogle place and a few cabins. Ogle's is a hundred yards from the road and fine for all three. Grotto Falls is not a toddler walk. Then the Gatlinburg Trail from Sugarlands, two miles each way on gravel, flat, stroller-fine, with the river the whole way.

Plan B: the high road is gated

Newfound Gap Road closes for ice with no warning, and Kuwohi Road closes with it. The sign is at Sugarlands. If it's shut, don't wait it out; turn around and do this.

  • 8:45a
    Roaring Fork Motor Nature Trail ★ 4.83k Directions
    Traffic light #8, Historic Nature Trail, then the one-way loop. The Noah Ogle place is a hundred yards from the road and fine for all three. Grotto Falls is not a toddler walk. Closes Monday, so this is its last weekend too.
  • 10:30a
    Gatlinburg Trail from Sugarlands ★ 4.610 Directions
    Two miles of flat gravel along the river. Stroller works. Turn around whenever.
  • 12:30p
    Home, lunch, naps
  • 5:45p
    Shadrack's drive-through lights Directions
    Same as Plan A. Kids in car seats, pajamas, radio synced. $39.95 a car on Friday. Leave by 5:15 for the Sevierville traffic.

Bail-out: Sugarlands Visitor Center ★ 4.72k Directions

Bottom of the mountain, on the way home from either plan. Warm, free, restrooms, the museum. If the summit was too cold for the baby or the road was too much for the two-year-old's stomach, this is where the day recovers.
