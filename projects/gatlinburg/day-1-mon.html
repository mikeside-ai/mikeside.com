The plan

Plan A

Monday before Thanksgiving is the lightest day the park will have all week. Park opens at 10. Be in the lot at 9:15. Parking is $25. Tickets: $94.99 adults, $84.99 for Cash. Slavik and Snejana are free. Buy online tonight, not at the gate.

  • 9:15a
    Park, tram to the gate, measure Cash Directions
    2700 Dollywood Parks Blvd, Pigeon Forge
    The measuring station on Showstreet gives him a wristband for the day. 42 inches opens Blazing Fury and Sky Rider. 48 opens the real coasters. Don't guess, measure.
  • 10:00a
    Straight to Wildwood Grove
    Back of the park, so go first while everyone else stops at the front. Frogs and Fireflies, Treetop Tower, the Great Tree Swing at 36 inches, and the splash-free Wildwood Creek playground. This is Slavik's whole park.
  • 11:30a
    Dollywood Express
    The steam train. No height minimum, twenty minutes sitting down, and the single best thing for Snejana. Ride it before lunch while she's still happy.
  • 12:15p
    Lunch, then the Village
    Carousel and Lemon Twist have no minimum. Santa is in the Imagination Playhouse near the train depot. Cash and one adult peel off for Blazing Fury and whatever his wristband allows. Use Parent Swap so nobody waits twice.
  • 2:30p
    Stroller nap loop
    Snejana and probably Slavik go down in the stroller. Walk slow through Craftsman's Valley. One adult takes Cash to the Country Fair rides, which are 36 inches and up.
  • 5:00p
    Dinner in the park, then the lights come on
    Snow in the Grove runs in Wildwood Grove each evening with the characters. Glacier Ridge and Peppermint Valley are the light walks. Get through the Grove first, then Glacier Ridge on the way out.
  • 6:45p
    Leave before the park does
    The 9pm close is for people without a one-year-old. Out by seven, home by 7:45, bed. Tomorrow is a recovery day on purpose.

Heights will decide Cash's day

No minimum: Dollywood Express, Village Carousel, Lemon Twist, Flying Elephants, Rockin' Roadway. Treetop Tower and Frogs and Fireflies need a 14+ companion under 42 and 36 inches. The Country Fair kiddie rides moved to a 36-inch minimum for 2026, so Slavik may be shut out of Busy Bees and Lucky Ducky. At 42 inches Cash gets Blazing Fury, Sky Rider and the Waltzing Swinger. At 48 inches he gets Thunderhead, Mystery Mine and Barnstormer. A seven-year-old is usually right around 48, so measure him at the cabin Sunday night and again at the park.

Plan B: rain all day

Steady rain in the forecast strip for Monday. Dollywood in a cold rain with a one-year-old is a bad trade for $300. Swap Dollywood to Tuesday or Wednesday on the hub and do this instead.

  • 9:00a
    Ripley's Aquarium of the Smokies Directions
    88 River Rd. Be there at opening. Penguins, the shark tunnel, touch tank for Cash. Two hours, all indoors, stroller fine. Garage next door.
  • 11:30a
    Home, lunch, naps
  • 3:30p
    Sugarlands Visitor Center museum Directions
    Free, warm, the stuffed bears, a ranger for Cash. Buy the weekly parking tag while you're there.
  • 5:30p
    Winterfest lights from the car Directions
    Pigeon Forge Parkway, five miles of lights, pajamas and hot chocolate. Costs nothing and nobody gets wet.

Bail-out: Dollywood's Parent Swap and the train Directions

If a kid melts down mid-park, the Dollywood Express is a twenty-minute sit-down that resets everybody. If it's the weather, the covered shows in the Village run all afternoon.
