The plan

Plan A

Everything today sits in two spots: the Old Mill square and The Island, three minutes apart. Iron Mountain opens at 9 and the forge sessions book up, so call (865) 365-4344 today and reserve two: a railroad spike knife for Vlad, a nail knife for Cash.

  • 9:00a
    Iron Mountain Metal Craft ★ 4.7551 Directions
    172 Old Mill Ave, Pigeon Forge
    Vlad hammers a railroad spike into a knife, about an hour, $85, ages 13 and up. Cash does the nail knife, ages 5 to 12, $20, with a smith at his elbow the whole time. Closed-toe shoes for both. A sheath is included; engraving is $10 extra and worth it for his.
  • 9:15a
    Allison, Slavik and Snejana: the Old Mill square Directions
    While the forge runs. The water wheel on the Little Pigeon River, ducks off the dam, the Old Mill General Store with the corn fritter samples, the candy kitchen. All flat, all stroller.
  • 10:45a
    Patriot Park, next door ★ 4.64k Directions
    236 Old Mill Ave. Grass and a riverside path to burn off the forge adrenaline. Free parking here if the mill lot is full.
  • 11:15a
    Lunch: Pottery House Café ★ 4.78k Directions
    3341 Old Mill St, across the footbridge. Opens at 11; be at the door, the wait is 30 minutes by noon. Fresh bread, chicken pot pie, kids' mac. The Old Mill Restaurant next door is the bigger, slower alternative.
  • 12:45p
    Home, naps
    Twenty minutes on the Spur. Long nap. Tonight is late-ish.
  • 3:45p
    The Island in Pigeon Forge ★ 4.765k Directions
    131 The Island Dr
    Free parking, free to walk in. The Flying Horse carousel, the Great Smoky Mountain Wheel ($18.99, $13.99 for Cash, ask about Snejana), Santa photos by the wheel, a mile of Winterfest lights. Ben and Jerry's. It is the easiest three hours in Pigeon Forge with three kids.
  • 5:30p
    Dinner at The Island
    Timberwood Grill or whatever has no line. Eat at 5:30; by 6:30 everything has a wait.
  • 6:00p
    Fountain show, then home
    The Winterfest fountain show runs at 6, 7, 8 and 9 in the courtyard with the three LED trees. Watch the 6 o'clock from a bench, then the Spur home. Bed by 7:30.

Who forges what

Iron Mountain's rules: the railroad spike knife is 13 and up, the horseshoe is 9 and up, the pony shoe is a smaller knife at $35, and the nail knife is 5 to 12 at $20. Cash qualifies for the nail knife only; don't argue it up a tier, the smith won't. Slavik and Snejana watch from behind the rope or walk the square with Allison. Sessions take 15 minutes of your hammering plus the smith's finishing; plan an hour for both of you. Reviewers' consensus is that this is the thing their kids talked about all the way home.

Plan B: cold rain, keep everything indoors

The forge is indoors and still happens. Swap the outdoor half for Pigeon Forge Snow, where the boys tube and Snejana watches from the warm side, and skip The Island until it clears.

  • 9:00a
    Iron Mountain Metal Craft ★ 4.7551 Directions
    Same as Plan A. The forge doesn't care about rain.
  • 10:45a
    Pigeon Forge Snow ★ 4.44k Directions
    2533 Teaster Ln, five minutes. Indoor tubing, $29.99 an hour, 38 inches and 3 years to ride, so both boys, Slavik on an adult's lap. Reviewers say the separate 'snow play' room is a slab of ice, so skip that add-on; Snejana watches the tubes from the lounge.
  • 12:15p
    Lunch at the Old Mill, then naps ★ 4.78k Directions
    Pottery House if the wait is short, Old Mill Restaurant if it isn't. Home by 1:15.
  • 4:00p
    WonderWorks Toddler Town, or the aquarium ★ 4.311k Directions
    WonderWorks (100 Music Rd) has a soft-play toddler zone and the science floor for Cash; loud and busy, go knowing that. Ripley's Aquarium is the calmer indoor option if you haven't used it.
  • 6:00p
    Winterfest lights from the car on the way home Directions
    The Parkway, slow, in pajamas.

Bail-out: The Old Mill General Store Directions

Right on the square, warm, candy counter, the water wheel outside the window. If the forge runs long or a kid is done, this is where the other adult waits with the little two.
