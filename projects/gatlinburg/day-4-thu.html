The plan

Plan A

Dollywood is closed and most of the town is at a buffet. Ober in the morning for the bears and the tubing hill, the big meal at the cabin at four, and the chairlift lights if everyone is still standing.

  • 9:45a
    Ober Mountain tram from downtown ★ 4.113k Directions
    Tram base at 1001 Parkway. $29 round trip for ages 5 and up, Slavik and Snejana ride free. Strollers fold and come aboard. Opening hours vary by day in early season, so check the night before.
  • 10:30a
    Wildlife Habitat first
    Black bears, otters and raccoons behind glass. The one thing up here all three can do, and the bears are most active in the morning.
  • 11:00a
    Snow tubing: Cash, Slavik and one adult ★ 4.113k Directions
    The tubing park opened for the season Nov 20 and runs all weather. Day tickets are good 11 to 5 with unlimited runs: a 350-foot hill, a 50-foot drop, and a moving carpet back up. At seven Cash rides alone and has to be 42 inches, so measure him first. Slavik rides on a ticketed adult's lap; Ober lists the little-kid ticket as ages 3 and 4 for about $3, so he's a big three-year-old at the window. Snejana stays with the other adults at the bears and the rink. Tubing tickets do not include the tram. Thanksgiving is a holiday price, about $50 for Cash and the adult, and it sells out: buy online tonight. Secured shoes and waterproof gloves for both boys, no food or drink on the hill.
  • 12:15p
    Tennessee Flyer mountain coaster, both boys
    Loads beside the tubing hill. Ages 3 and up, passenger seat 38 inches, so Cash and Slavik each ride in front of an adult who works the brake levers. Two per cart, 300 lb limit, 3,750 feet of track, 25 mph if you let it. $18 a ride, or buy the Adventure Pass for unlimited coaster plus the tram, bears and skating. Closes in ice. Skating only if there's still time and the Fun Pass covers it.
  • 1:15p
    Tram down, home for naps
    Snack lunch on the way. Everyone down by 2.
  • 4:00p
    Thanksgiving dinner at the cabin
    A turkey breast, not a turkey. Cabin ovens are small and unreliable. Sides from Food City Wednesday. If you'd rather not cook, the backup is below, and it needs booking before you leave Birmingham.
  • 5:30p
    Lights Over Gatlinburg from the SkyLift ★ 4.616k Directions
    765 Parkway, 15 minutes from the table. Included with SkyPark admission, runs nightly. Each chair holds three heartbeats and the baby counts, so you're two chairs. No strollers anywhere in the park. Snejana rides in the carrier on a lap. Skip this if dinner ran long. It's a bonus, not a plan.
  • 7:00p
    Home and down

If you want someone else to cook

Mama's Farmhouse, 208 Pickel St, Pigeon Forge Directions. Family-style, all you can eat, a special Thanksgiving menu, open 11 to 9. The right call for this crew because food arrives in bowls immediately and nobody needs a menu. Reservations are encouraged and it fills up. Book it today as the backup even if you plan to cook. Ober Mountain runs a Thanksgiving Feast at the top, 11 to 5, which would fold the whole morning into one trip; reservations there too. Cracker Barrel on the Pigeon Forge Parkway serves its traditional plate from 11 with no reservation, and The Park Grill in Gatlinburg has a special menu 11:30 to 9. The Pigeon Forge trolley doesn't run on Thanksgiving, so whatever you pick, you're driving.

Plan B: nobody cooks

The cabin oven is a toy, or the morning ran long, or you'd just rather not. The reservation at Mama's Farmhouse is the backup; book it before you leave Birmingham.

  • 9:45a
    Ober Mountain tram from downtown ★ 4.113k Directions
    Same morning as Plan A: bears, tubing and the coaster for the boys, skating if there's time.
  • 12:30p
    Home, naps
    Short ones. Everyone up by 2:15.
  • 3:00p
    Mama's Farmhouse, Pigeon Forge ★ 4.310k Directions
    208 Pickel St. Family style, all you can eat, turkey and the fixings arrive in bowls before anyone can complain. Open 11 to 9. Reservations encouraged, so this slot assumes you booked.
  • 5:00p
    Winterfest lights on the way home Directions
    You're already on the Pigeon Forge Parkway. Drive it slow in the dark, then the Spur home. Nobody needs to get out of the car.

Bail-out: Cracker Barrel, Pigeon Forge ★ 4.46k Directions

If the reservation falls through or you decide at two o'clock that you want someone else to cook, Cracker Barrel on the Parkway serves the Thanksgiving plate from 11 with no reservation. It will have a wait. It will also have a kids' menu and a fire.
