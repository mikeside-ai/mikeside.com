The plan

Plan A

Nobody drives far today. Short walk in the park, long nap, then the mountaintop for the lights.

  • 9:00a
    Sugarlands Visitor Center ★ 4.72k Directions
    1420 Fighting Creek Gap Rd (US-441)
    Two miles south of town on US-441. Buy the $15 weekly parking tag here and put it on the dash. Free museum inside with the stuffed animals, which buys you twenty minutes if it's cold.
  • 9:30a
    Cataract Falls ★ 4.62k Directions
    Starts behind the visitor center
    The trail starts behind the visitor center. Under half a mile, flat, dirt. The shortest walk to a waterfall in the park. Cash walks, Slavik walks most of it, Snejana rides in the carrier.
  • 10:15a
    Sugarlands Valley Nature Trail Directions
    Newfound Gap Rd, 0.3 mi south of the visitor center
    Half a mile, fully paved, flat. The one trail in the park where the stroller actually works. Creek, old chimney, done by eleven.
  • 11:30a
    Home, lunch, naps
    Protect this. Tonight runs late.
  • 3:15p
    Park downtown, Chondola to Anakeesta ★ 4.218k Directions
    Anakeesta base: 576 Parkway. Garage: McMahan, traffic light #3
    Base is at 576 Parkway. Use the McMahan garage at traffic light #3 or the garage across from the aquarium. The gondola cabins fit a stroller, so bring it up. Adults $34.99, Cash $22.99, the little two free. Buy online.
  • 3:45p
    Treehouse play area and Vista Gardens in daylight
    The Treehouse Adventure play structure is the toddler payoff. Treetop Skywalk is included but no strollers and no carrying, so one adult does it with Cash while the other stays at the playground.
  • 5:15p
    Lights come on
    Enchanted Christmas is the whole mountaintop lit up. Half an hour of walking it is plenty. Cliff Top has food if you'd rather eat up there than fight downtown.
  • 6:15p
    Down and home
    The Chondola line down gets long after 6:30. Be in it by 6:15.

Plan B: cold soaking rain

Anakeesta runs in rain but the whole mountaintop is outdoors and the Skywalk closes in wind. Below 45 and wet, do this.

  • 9:30a
    Ripley's Aquarium of the Smokies Directions
    If you did not use it Monday. Otherwise the Arts and Crafts show at the convention center opens today.
  • 12:00p
    Home, lunch, naps
  • 3:30p
    Great Smoky Thanksgiving Arts & Crafts Show ★ 4.62k Directions
    Convention center, 234 Historic Nature Trail. Free, indoor, wide aisles. An easy hour.
  • 5:30p
    SkyPark lights if it clears, cabin movie if not ★ 4.616k Directions
    The chairlift runs in light rain. Carrier for Snejana, two chairs. Otherwise popcorn and bed.

Bail-out: The Chondola down ★ 4.218k Directions

Anakeesta's gondola runs continuously. If someone is done, one adult rides down with them and the other stays with the rest. The lodge at the base has restrooms and a coffee counter.
