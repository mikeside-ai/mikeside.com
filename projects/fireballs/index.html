    For the sideline

    How U7 games work

    From the league's U7/U8 rules. Coaches teach these all season, so expect a few quick stops for lessons.

    The basics

    • Four 10-minute quarters with short breaks
    • 5 on 5, goalies included
    • Goalies rotate so every kid gets a turn
    • No heading. No slide tackles.
    • No pushing or shoving. Normal contact is fine.

    Restarts

    • Over the sideline: throw-in. Two hands, ball behind the head, feet down.
    • Other team kicks it over your end line: your goal kick
    • Your team kicks it over your own end line: their corner kick
    • Handball: free kick for the other team

    Build-out line

    When a goalie has the ball, or on a goal kick, the other team must jog back to the half line. The goalie can play it right away. This gives kids room to pass out from the back.

    Goalie hands

    Only the goalie uses hands, and only inside the box. The first slip or two is a warning while they learn.
