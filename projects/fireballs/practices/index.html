Fireballs · U7 Boys · Fall 2026

Practice plan

Thursdays, 5:30 to 6:30 PM. Eight practices, each with a focus and four suggested drills that build week to week. The next practice is open below.

Coach and helper tips

Keep talking short, about 20 seconds, then let them play. Give water breaks between drills. Bring cones, pinnies in two colors, a ball pump and a size 3 ball for every player.

Every drill should give each kid a ball when possible, keep lines short, and end in a game.
