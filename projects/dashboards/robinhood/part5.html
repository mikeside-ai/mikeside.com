Why this section exists. Earnings are the one risk on this page you can see coming with a date attached. 58% of your equity book walks into a scheduled binary event in the next month, and the two largest are your two largest positions: RKLB and PLTR. Read that alongside the stress lab. A 20% single-name gap on a position that size is a $2,557 hole, and you are carrying it with essentially no margin cushion — which is the specific mechanism by which a bad print becomes a forced sale at the worst possible price rather than a bad week you ride out. You do not have to trade the earnings. You do have to decide, before the date rather than after it, whether you are willing to hold that gap risk on borrowed money.

Dates and consensus EPS come from Robinhood’s earnings calendar as of Jul 24, 2026 and companies move them, sometimes with a few days’ notice — confirm anything you intend to trade around. “am” means before the open, “pm” after the close, so an after-close print moves the next session. Consensus is an estimate of a single quarterly number, not a forecast of the stock: names on this list have historically moved on guidance, bookings and cash burn far more than on the EPS line, and several of your holdings are pre-revenue enough that the estimate is close to meaningless. Positions without a scheduled date either do not report in this window or are ETFs, warrants or foreign listings the calendar does not cover.
