Dates and consensus EPS come from Robinhood’s earnings calendar as of Jul 31, 2026 and companies move them, sometimes with a few days’ notice — confirm anything you intend to trade around. “am” means before the open, “pm” after the close, so an after-close print moves the next session. Consensus is an estimate of a single quarterly number, not a forecast of the stock: names on this list have historically moved on guidance, bookings and cash burn far more than on the EPS line, and several of your holdings are pre-revenue enough that the estimate is close to meaningless. Positions without a scheduled date either do not report in this window or are ETFs, warrants or foreign listings the calendar does not cover.
