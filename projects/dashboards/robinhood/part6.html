Why this section exists. Earnings are the one risk on this page you can see coming with a date attached. 47% of your equity book walks into a scheduled binary event in the next month, and the two largest are your two largest positions: RKLB and NVDA. Read that alongside the stress lab. A 20% single-name gap on a position that size is a $2,979 hole, and you are carrying it on borrowed money. The August 4 move rebuilt real distance to the maintenance line — the book can now fall roughly a quarter before a 25% call — so a single bad print is no longer an immediate forced-sale mechanism the way it was a week ago. It is still a gap you take at full leverage, and the cushion you are spending is the one PLTR just handed you. You do not have to trade the earnings. You do have to decide, before the date rather than after it, whether you are willing to hold that gap risk on borrowed money.

Dates and consensus EPS come from Robinhood’s earnings calendar as of Aug 4, 2026 and companies move them, sometimes with a few days’ notice — confirm anything you intend to trade around. “am” means before the open, “pm” after the close, so an after-close print moves the next session. Consensus is an estimate of a single quarterly number, not a forecast of the stock: names on this list have historically moved on guidance, bookings and cash burn far more than on the EPS line, and several of your holdings are pre-revenue enough that the estimate is close to meaningless. Positions without a scheduled date either do not report in this window or are ETFs, warrants or foreign listings the calendar does not cover.
