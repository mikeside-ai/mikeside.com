Earnings are the one risk on this page you can see coming with a date attached. 19% of your equity book walks into a scheduled binary event in the next month, and by far the largest is your largest position: NVDA on Aug 26, with PL and PANW following in early September. LUNR — last month’s marquee risk — reported Aug 13 and is behind you. Read that alongside the stress lab. A 20% single-name gap on NVDA is a $2,025 hole, and you are carrying it on borrowed money. The August 4 move rebuilt real distance to the maintenance line — the book can still fall roughly a quarter before a 25% call — so a single bad print is no longer an immediate forced-sale mechanism. You do have to decide, before the date rather than after it, whether you are willing to hold that gap risk on borrowed money.
