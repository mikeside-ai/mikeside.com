Privacy policy

Last updated August 24, 2026. The short version: this site and its bots publish setlists. They do not collect, store, or sell anything about you.

What this covers

mikeside.com and the automated accounts it operates: @SetlistLizard and @SetlistHound on X, and any companion accounts on Instagram. All are fan projects, not affiliated with any band or platform.

What we collect from visitors

Nothing. This site has no accounts, no sign-ups, no comment forms, no analytics scripts of our own, no advertising, and it sets no cookies. Our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (IP address, requested page, timestamp) for security and operations, as virtually all web hosts do; we do not use them to identify anyone.

What the bots collect

Also nothing about people. The bots read publicly published setlist data (from sources credited on each project's page), format it, and post it to their own accounts. They do not read your posts, followers, messages, or profile, and they do not track who views or interacts with theirs. Any interaction data you see (likes, replies) lives on the platform you used, under that platform's own privacy policy.

Third parties

Posts are published through the official APIs of X and Meta (Instagram). Your interactions with those posts are governed by X's privacy policy and Meta's privacy policy. We receive nothing about you from either platform beyond what is publicly visible on our own posts.

Children

The site and bots are not directed at children and, collecting nothing, collect nothing from them.

Changes

If any of the above ever changes — for example, if a project is added that does collect something — this page will say so plainly, with the date updated above.
