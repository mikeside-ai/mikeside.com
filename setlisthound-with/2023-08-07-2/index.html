Surprise acoustic show in the barn. Mr. Tooker was played in an alternate arrangement & featured improvised lyrics. Jimmy broke a string & Brian switched to mandolin during China Cat Sunflower. Charlie Carrier joined the band for Let U Go through Johnny B. Goode. Before Big River, the band spontaneously changed to Charlie Carrier on electric guitar, Sam Bradley on mandolin, John Thomas on acoustic guitar, Jeremy Kaplan on accordion, Ian Fay on bass, and Joe Babick on cajon.
